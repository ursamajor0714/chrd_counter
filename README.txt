# CHAD 1000X Step Counter

A simple mobile web app that tracks step-up exercises using your smartphone's motion sensors.

## Features
- Automatic step counting using motion sensors
- Real-time progress tracking toward 1,000 steps
- Exercise timer
- Manual step adjustment
- Voice announcements at milestones
- Mobile-friendly interface

## How to Use
1. Open the app on your smartphone.
2. Place your phone in your pocket.
3. Allow motion sensor access.
4. Press **Start** and begin your step-up exercise.
5. Track your progress toward 1,000 steps.

## Requirements
- Smartphone with motion sensors
- Modern mobile browser
- HTTPS connection
